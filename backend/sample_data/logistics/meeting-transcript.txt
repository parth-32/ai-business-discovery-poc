Meeting Notes: QuickShip Logistics Operations Review
Date: 2026-08-10
Attendees: Dave (Operations Manager), Sarah (Customer Success), Marcus (Lead Dispatcher)

Dave: Thanks for joining. We need to address our delivery tracking issues. Last month our SLA compliance dropped to 78%. Customers are complaining about missing packages and late updates.

Sarah: Exactly. Support is getting overwhelmed with "Where is my order?" calls. Right now, customers only get an email when the order is created, and nothing until it's delivered—or fails to deliver.

Marcus: The problem is how dispatch works right now. Drivers text me updates on WhatsApp or call me when they hit traffic. I manually type those updates into the master Excel sheet (`Delivery_Tracker_2026.xlsx`). Half the time I'm too busy assigning new routes to update the spreadsheet.

Dave: So drivers have no mobile app to update status directly?

Marcus: None. They write delivery times on paper clipboards and hand them in at the end of the shift. If a package gets lost or damaged at 11 AM, we don't know until 6 PM when the driver comes back.

Sarah: Customers want real-time visibility. If Uber Eats can show where a pizza is, why can't we show where a $500 package is? Also, drivers need a way to upload proof of delivery (photo or signature) immediately.

Dave: What about emergency rerouting? Yesterday a truck broke down on I-95 and Marcus spent two hours calling drivers individually to reassign 40 packages.

Marcus: A automated alert system for delayed routes would save hours every single day.
