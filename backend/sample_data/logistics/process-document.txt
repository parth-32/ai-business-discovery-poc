Standard Operating Procedure: Delivery Operations & Status Updates
Document ID: SOP-OPS-2026-V2

1. Order Ingestion & Routing
1.1 Orders arrive via customer web portal or phone sales.
1.2 Operations Manager manually exports orders to CSV at 7:00 AM daily.
1.3 Lead Dispatcher assigns packages to driver routes using Excel spreadsheet sorting.
1.4 Printed route manifests are distributed to drivers at 7:30 AM depot loading.

2. Delivery Execution & Status Reporting
2.1 Drivers proceed along assigned route manifest.
2.2 Status changes (In Transit, Delivered, Exception) are communicated via WhatsApp group or phone call to Dispatcher.
2.3 Dispatcher manually updates `Delivery_Tracker_2026.xlsx` master workbook.
2.4 In case of failed delivery or damaged goods, driver records reason code on paper clipboard.

3. Proof of Delivery (POD) Handling
3.1 Driver collects physical signature on paper receipt slip.
3.2 Physical slips are returned to warehouse intake office at end of shift (17:00 - 18:00).
3.3 Intake staff scan paper slips into PDF archive once per week.

4. Known Process Deficiencies & Risks
- Average status latency: 2 to 6 hours between physical delivery and system update.
- POD retrieval latency: Up to 5 business days for customer dispute resolution.
- Route exception notification: Manual phone cascade; no automated driver re-routing.
